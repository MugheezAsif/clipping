# Instagram Reels kit: $500K Experiences

**How to post:**
- Upload from `clips/`.
- **Cover:** `thumbnails/…__Shorts_cover_9x16.jpg`.
- Paste the caption; the first line is the hook.
- Add the alt text under Advanced settings.
- **Optional:** post as a Trial Reel first.
- **One per day:** 03 → 02 → 04 → 05 → 01.
- **Credits:** clips 01 and 02 need the 🔊 credit line.
