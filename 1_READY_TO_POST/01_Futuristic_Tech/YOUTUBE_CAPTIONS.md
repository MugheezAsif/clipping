# YouTube Shorts posting kit

Copy and paste straight from the grey boxes. **Thumbnails** are in `thumbnails/`: the 9:16 Shorts cover and the 16:9 YouTube thumbnail. Each clip has:
- **3 title options.** Use option 1 first. If a clip stalls, re-test the same clip with another title later on a different account or platform.
- **A description** (with the required credits)
- **A pinned comment.** Post it yourself right after uploading, then pin it.

---

## Before you post: quick rules

1. **Post one clip per day**, at roughly the same time. Suggested order (strongest debate and awe first):
   1. **04 Lab Chicken**
   2. **03 Flying Car**
   3. **05 Blowtorch**
   4. **01 Neuralink**
   5. **02 Kai's Arm**
2. **Titles stay short.** The Shorts feed only shows the first ~40 characters, so the hook is at the front.
3. **Keep the credit lines** in every description. Clips 02 and 03 also need the sound-effect credit; it's already included below.
4. **Don't change the video length.** All clips are under 60 s on purpose, because YouTube blocks Shorts over 60 s that get a copyright claim.
5. **Copyright:** this is MrBeast's footage. Posting outside his official clipping program (Vyro) can get a clip claimed or demonetized. That's normal for fan clip channels, but know it going in.
6. **After 24 h, 3 days and 7 days**, write down the views and "viewed vs. swiped away" for each clip in `2_RESEARCH_AND_KNOWLEDGE/CLIPPING_KNOWLEDGE.md` (section 12), so we learn what works.

## Upload settings (YouTube Studio)

- **Format:** the files are already vertical 1080×1920, so YouTube will detect them as Shorts automatically.
- **Audience:** "No, it's not made for kids."
- **Category:** Entertainment (01 and 05 can also be Science & Technology).
- **Thumbnail:** Shorts use a frame from the video. Pick the frame where the hook text and a face are both visible.
- **Comments:** leave on, and post and pin your comment right away. Early replies help the first test audience engage.
